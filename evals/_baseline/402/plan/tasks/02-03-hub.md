## Interactions
- Recurring series (`todo done`, `todo skip`): notes drop on the next occurrence, stay on a skipped item (spec open question 10).
- `todo sync`: notes travel in the whole item; two sides adding notes offline keep only the later side's list (same limit as tags).
- `todo export`: no notes in CSV or Markdown.
- `todo show` prints `parent` (ticket 01), `snoozed until` and `blocked on` from the fields ticket 01 added.
- Non-printable text: notes show `?` like titles.
- Exit codes: unknown id on `note`/`show` exits 2.
- `updated_at`: adding a note stamps the item.
