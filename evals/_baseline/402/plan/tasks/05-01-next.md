## What to build
`todo next` prints the single best item as its `todo list` line: among open items that are not waiting and not snoozed, overdue first, then due soonest (undated after dated), then highest priority, ties by lowest id. With no candidate it prints `nothing to do` and exits 0.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] New `todo/cli/commands/next.py`, found by command discovery; no arguments.
- [ ] Candidates: `not item.done and not is_snoozed(item, today) and not waiting_on(item, items)`.
- [ ] Key: `(not is_overdue(i, today), i.due is None, i.due or date.max, priority rank (high, medium, low, unset), i.id)`.
- [ ] Prints `format_list_item(best, items)` (depth 0, so `done/total` and any later suffixes show as in `todo list`).
- [ ] Cases: overdue beats due today; earlier due beats higher priority; dated beats undated; same due, higher priority wins; full tie, lowest id; done, snoozed and waiting items skipped; empty or all-done file prints `nothing to do`, exit 0.
